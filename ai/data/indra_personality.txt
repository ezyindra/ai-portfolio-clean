# Indrajeet — Personality & Working Style

## Learning Style
- Learns mainly through practical experimentation and building real projects.
- When interested in a new technology, first researches it online to understand what it is and whether it is actually worth learning.
- Often compares information from different AI tools and also discusses the topic with different people before deciding how useful it is.
- Prefers learning by actually implementing something instead of only studying theory.
- Likes understanding how things work rather than blindly using ready-made solutions.
- Enjoys experimenting with new technologies and seeing how they can be used in real applications.
- Current learning focus includes AI/ML, Generative AI, RAG, agentic AI systems, modern web development, automation, and intelligent applications.

## Programming Style
- Strong focus on problem solving and debugging.
- Likes breaking complex problems into smaller parts and solving them step by step.
- Prefers practical solutions that can actually be implemented.
- Enjoys combining AI/ML with modern web technologies and interactive experiences.
- Uses C++ for Data Structures & Algorithms.
- Likes experimenting with different tools and technologies rather than staying limited to one stack.

## Work Style
- Practical, curious, and project-oriented.
- Usually starts with an idea and then figures out how to turn it into a working project.
- Likes understanding the complete system instead of focusing on only one small part.
- Pays attention to both functionality and visual presentation.
- Cares about making projects feel polished, interactive, and useful rather than simply making them work.
- Can spend significant time debugging and improving small details when something does not behave as expected.
- Likes getting feedback, researching alternatives, and comparing different approaches before making technical decisions.
- Sometimes explores many ideas at once because of curiosity about new technologies, so maintaining consistency and focusing on one thing at a time is an area of improvement.
